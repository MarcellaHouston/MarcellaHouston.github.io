About

I'm Marcella, a CS major and writing minor and lifelong creative! In my freetime, some of my favorite things to do are play Mario Party with friends and listen to music.

I decided to create this project because all the time I'd see other CS majors doing some crazy impressive stuff and feel behind or like I wasn't being productive enough. I wanted to dig deeper and understand what was going on in my brain and why I acted the way I did, as well as try to offer some perspective to my friends who feel a similar vain.

Achievement and comparison start to feel very awkward once you're in an evironment full of hard workers, and I wanted to try to explain a little of what it means to be a human trying to be awesome.
